# Third-party components

TrustDive does not redistribute the FineDiving videos or large RICA² model
checkpoints.

- FineDiving data and annotations: obtain them from the
  [FineDiving project](https://github.com/xujinglin/FineDiving) and follow its
  stated access and citation terms.
- RICA²: obtain the implementation and required pretrained resources from the
  [RICA² project](https://github.com/abrarmajeedi/rica2_aqa) and follow its
  license and dependency instructions.

The v10 comparison also includes lightweight matched-feature baselines inspired
by group-aware contrastive regression (CoRe; ICCV 2021) and the procedure-aware
FineDiving/TSA formulation (CVPR 2022). These implementations share TrustDive's
frozen RICA²/I3D representations and are not copies of, or substitutes for, the
authors' official end-to-end systems.

Files generated by TrustDive from these resources are provided only where they
are needed to audit the published numerical results. They do not replace the
original datasets or third-party software.
